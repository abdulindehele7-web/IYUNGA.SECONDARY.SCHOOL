About Our School

Iyunga Secondary School is committed to providing quality education in a safe, supportive and disciplined learning environment.

We encourage students to develop academic knowledge, practical skills, creativity, confidence and good character.

Our goal is to prepare students for higher education, employment and responsible participation in society.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence that produces knowledgeable, disciplined, responsible and innovative students.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and practical opportunities that develop knowledge, skills, discipline and character.
